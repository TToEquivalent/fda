About the group

Seeing the hidden dynamics of molecules.

The Ullrich Group is part of the Department of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Georgia. Our research uses time-resolved spectroscopic experiments to distinguish the intrinsic properties of molecules from the effects of their surroundings.

By combining carefully controlled experiments with theoretical calculations, we investigate how molecular systems respond to light and how their structure and environment influence the pathways that follow.
